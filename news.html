Collaborative Approaches in Behavioural Science: Cross Sector Partnerships for Equitable Health Research and Practice

Dr Faith Matcham, University of Sussex, UK

Dr Matcham presented at the UK Society for Behavioural Medicine Annual Scientific Meeting as part of a symposium on collaborative approaches in behavioural science. Her contribution focused on co-developing public engagement workshops on loneliness with older adults from ethnically diverse communities, in partnership with trusted community organisations. This work highlights how culturally and linguistically adapted, community-led approaches can reveal forms of "hidden loneliness" often missed by standard research models. The symposium brought together interdisciplinary perspectives on building equitable health research through meaningful collaboration, trust, and shared power.

THRIVE Lab presents digital phenotyping research for MDD at Get Digital colloquium

Faith Matcham (PI) shares insights on predicting daily mood in depression using ambulatory assessment data

Last week members of the THRIVE Lab took part in the Get Digital colloquium, where Faith Matcham (PI) presented our research on predicting day‑to‑day mood in Major Depressive Disorder (MDD) using digital phenotyping and ambulatory assessment data.

The meeting brought together researchers from psychology, psychiatry, data science, and digital health, and highlighted both the promise and the growing pains of this rapidly developing field. As access to intensive, real‑world data increases, so too does the need for careful thinking about how these data are analysed, interpreted, and ultimately used.

A recurring message across the discussions was that meaningful progress will depend on stronger shared foundations — particularly around how daily mood and related constructs are defined, measured, and reported over time. For work like ours, which aims to understand and predict mood dynamics in everyday life, these issues are central to ensuring findings are interpretable, comparable, and clinically relevant.

Dr Faith Matcham joins Scientific Programme Committee for the 40th ECNP Congress

THRIVE group PI and Associate Professor of Psychology appointed to shape the 2027 programme in Barcelona

Dr Faith Matcham, THRIVE group PI and Associate Professor of Psychology, is serving on the Scientific Programme Committee for the 40th ECNP Congress in Barcelona (25–28 September 2027). Alongside colleagues from across neuroscience and psychiatry, she's helping build a scientific programme that focuses on genuine advances and emerging topics, without the usual conference fluff. The committee is also making a deliberate effort to include diverse voices and perspectives, both in the content and the speakers. With ECNP celebrating its 40th anniversary, Faith's contribution is part of what's shaping up to be a scientifically robust, innovative programme worth paying attention to.
