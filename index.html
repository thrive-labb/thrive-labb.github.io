SOCIETY FOR AMBULATORY ASSESSMENT (SAA)

Feasibility and Acceptability of Ecological Momentary Assessment (EMA) for Chronic Pain: Implications for Adaptive mHealth Interventions

Harding RP et al.

Presenter: Bex Harding
Event: Society for Ambulatory Assessment Conference 2026
Venue: Conference Programme (see link)
Series: Conference Presentation

This presentation examines the feasibility and acceptability of Ecological Momentary Assessment (EMA) for individuals living with chronic pain using a 14-day intensive data collection protocol.

High retention and response rates support the use of EMA to capture real-time pain, mood, sleep, and self-management behaviours, providing a foundation for developing personalised and adaptive mHealth interventions for chronic pain management.

Bidirectional associations between pain, anxiety, and depressive symptoms during adolescence: Findings from the Quebec Newborn Twin Study

Poster Presentation | SSM Annual Conference 2026

Dates: September 9th - 11th, 2026
Format: Poster Presentation
Study: Quebec Newborn Twin Study
Key Focus: Sex differences (male/female)
Event: SSM Annual Conference 2026

PhD student Hai Tran investigates the bidirectional associations between pain and internalising problems (anxiety and depressive symptoms) across adolescence, using both phenotypic and genetically sensitive cross-lagged panel models. The research draws on data from the Quebec Newborn Twin Study and tests whether the associations are moderated by birth sex (male, female).
